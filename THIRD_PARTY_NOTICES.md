# Third-party material

`simulation/ltspice/OPA1656.LIB` is the Texas Instruments OPA1656 macro-model,
Final 1.3 (25 August 2022). Its original copyright and model-use notices are
preserved in the file. TI product/model resources: https://www.ti.com/product/OPA1656

`simulation/ltspice/1N4148_ON.lib` retains the LTspice standard.dio 1N4148 model
entry identified as `mfg=OnSemi`, under the local name `D4148_ON`. It is simulation
model data, not a component specification or a constant-voltage diode model.

The archived report cites Electronic Circuits 2 course slides and the course
formula sheet. Those third-party course PDFs are not included in this repository.
Python packages and LTspice must be obtained separately under their own terms.

No blanket open-source license is assigned here. Repository privacy does not
replace third-party terms; review redistribution rights before public release.
